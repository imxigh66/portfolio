TransportDomus
stress-free.

Redesign
Website

Redesign of a landing page for a Canadian marketplace that connects people with movers, couriers, delivery and taxi drivers.

Role

UI design

Tasks

Page structure, visual redesign

Tools

Figma

Team

Solo project

01 / Context

Make the first screen do the work.

A landing page like this has a few seconds to explain the service and get people to request a quote.

I reworked the structure and the visual style so that the main action is clear from the very first screen.

02 / Approach

2 colour directions

Two directions, one choice.

01

Search first

A quote form right in the hero: service, from, to.

02

Trust

Ratings, reviews and verified professionals near the top.

03

Clear steps

Three simple steps explain how the service works.

03 / Solution

Everything transport, in one place.

Every section supports one action, requesting a quote, and leads the user from the promise to the form.

04 / Result

A clearer path to a quote.

The new page explains the service in one screen and keeps the quote form in focus. The two colour directions show how the same structure works with a different brand mood.
