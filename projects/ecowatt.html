UX/UI case · 2026

EcoWatt
energy, in lei.

Team project
Mobile app

A mobile app that shows people in Moldova what each appliance costs and how to pay less without giving up comfort. I designed the interface and developed the app in a team of three.

Role

Product design & development

Tasks

Research, user flows, UI, development

Tools

Figma, FigJam, React

Team

Team of 3

01 / Context

People see the bill, not the reason.

More than 550,000 apartments in Moldova get one final electricity bill a month. People don't know which appliance uses the most, what each one costs, or how to pay less without losing comfort.

The goal was a simple assistant that turns consumption data into lei and gives advice people can actually apply.

02 / Research

15 interviews · user persona

First, listen.

“The boiler and the fridge use the most. I can't just cut down, because I have small children at home.”

Svetlana, tenant in Chișinău, mother of two (persona based on interviews)

01

No breakdown

People only see the final bill and can't tell which device costs the most.

02

Small, random savings

Changing bulbs or heating less saved only 100–200 lei and felt like guessing.

03

Comfort first

Essential devices like the boiler and the fridge can't simply be turned off.

03 / Solution

Costs in lei, at a glance.

Instead of kWh charts, the app shows money: the monthly bill and its forecast, each appliance's share of it, and concrete recommendations with the savings attached.

04 / Result

Less guessing. Clear savings.

Every screen uses the same numbers, so a user goes from the bill to the device to the advice in a few taps. Next step: test the prototype with the people we interviewed.
