TravelFlow
trips, planned together.

Personal project
Web app

A web platform where travellers find places, plan routes with friends, track the budget and share their trips. Designed and built by me.

Role

Product design & development

Tasks

Research, UX, UI, front-end, back-end

Tools

Figma, React, .NET, OpenAI API

Team

Solo project

01 / Context

Too many apps for one trip.

Travellers use one app for reviews, another for routes, a spreadsheet for the budget and a chat to agree with friends. Most travel services solve only one narrow task.

The goal was one place for the whole trip: discovering places, planning together and sharing the experience afterwards.

02 / Research

4 competitors analysed

What others miss.

01

TripAdvisor

A huge base of reviews, focused on choosing places.

02

Polarsteps

Tracking and documenting trips that already happen.

03

Wanderlog & Sygic

Route planning and travel guides.

03 / Solution

One trip, one place.

Places are found by mood and interests instead of long lists. Each trip has an overview, a route on the map, notes and a shared budget, and friends can plan it together in real time.

04 / Result

Designed, built and working.

TravelFlow is a working product: a React front-end, a .NET 8 API, real-time chat, AI recommendations and three interface languages. Building it myself let me check every design decision in real code.
