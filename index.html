02 / About
✦

A designer who knows how products are built and tested.

I'm a product designer with a degree in Information Technology from the Technical University of Moldova. For several years I've been designing and building my own products, so I know how an interface goes from idea to working code.

That background shapes how I design. I think about edge cases, empty and error states, and how a screen will actually be built, so my designs are easy to use and easy to hand off to developers.
